# Runbook — Terakidz task boxes B2 on kodeme.io (founder-gated)

Takes roadmap row **B2** (Terakidz physical task boxes with QR codes that open app activities)
from **built-local** to boxes on sale: a stockable box product sold through the order-intake
path, a printed insert whose QR codes open companion activities in the Terakidz app, and the
suppliers, stock, couriers and channels a physical product needs.

This is a founder runbook. Read-only checks are **R**; production changes are **M**. Nothing in
this document has been applied by the implementation slice. Every target is the kodeme.io estate,
never idtpp.

Slice of record: `kodemeio-docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-28-teracorp-track-b-design.md` §4 "B2"
and `…/plans/2026-09-28-teracorp-track-b.md` slice TB2. Roadmap row: **B2**. Founder decision
**TB-D4 = (a)**: a QR is an **open link** to a companion activity — no single-use activation
code, because the physical box is the product. (b), per-box codes minted by Odoo and redeemed by
the app, is an L-size addition if link sharing is ever observed to cost sales.

## How the pieces fit

```
contracts/app_links/terakidz.v1.json ──gen_app_qr.py──▶ templates/terakidz-box-insert/assets/
        │  ids ⊆ catalog snapshot (free tier)                qr-<id>.png + own-work.json
        ▼                                                          │ asset:image-qr-terakidz-<id>
terakidz-spa /a/:activityId ◀── QR scan ── printed insert ◀── F3 render (released, expert → founder)
                                                                   │
product_lines/terakidz-box.yaml ──harness──▶ box product ─▶ order-intake principal (MCP prepare/execute)
                                              ─▶ confirm (distinct human approver) ─▶ delivery picking
                                              ─▶ PAY1 settlement ─▶ one paid invoice
```

The insert's image blocks render as labelled placeholders: factory_template fetches no image bytes
and records each licensed asset's public URL on the render (`asset_urls`) — the print shop's
manifest. The printer places the QR PNG from that URL at the placeholder.

## Regenerating the QR images (R, then a reviewed commit)

```bash
# kodemeio-dokploy — verify the committed images are exactly what the links file produces
uv run ops/app_links/gen_app_qr.py --links contracts/app_links/terakidz.v1.json \
    --out templates/terakidz-box-insert/assets --check
# the generator's own tests: byte-exact regeneration + zxing-cpp decode of every PNG
uv run --with qrcode==8.2 --with pillow --with zxing-cpp pytest ops/app_links/tests -q
```

Regenerate (drop `--check`) whenever the host or an id changes, and commit the PNGs and
`own-work.json` together. Every reprint of an insert needs the new images registered as **new**
assets (a verified asset's URL and sha are frozen).

## Local e2e (K2 harness)

TB2 adds two harness steps; the harness itself is kodemeio-odoo slice K2. The acceptance
database needs, beyond the harness's module set: `api_product`, `sale_integration_ref` (pulls
`api_sale` → `sale_stock` → `stock`) and `mcp_operation_binding` (pulls `mcp_base`).

```bash
# kodemeio-odoo, on its own acceptance DB only (the harness refuses any other name)
bin/product-line-acceptance terakidz-box --db odoo_test_tb_<name> --recreate
```

Pass criteria: L1–L4 and L10 (spec §3.3), L5–L8 not applicable (the line sells nothing
digitally), L9 not applicable (no launch), plus the `physical` checks in the result:

- the QR images registered as `owned` with the committed evidence and sha256 (`own_work_assets`).
  This needs the harness's bundle assembly to carry `own_work_assets`
  (`physical_assets.collect_own_work_assets`); until it does, the insert's images come only from
  the kit step's synthetic overlay and the `insert_images_have_own_work_evidence` check **fails
  closed**, so the run does not pass;
- the box is a storable product; the order is created by the order-intake principal through the
  MCP gateway's prepare/execute (draft, auto-approved), and a replay creates no second order;
- the confirm waits for a human, the principal cannot approve its own, a distinct approver does;
- exactly one outgoing picking for the box;
- PAY1's fake charges once, the signed settlement pays exactly one invoice, the replay changes
  nothing;
- the insert render is released and records every own-work QR image it places.

## Operational gates (founder)

Every gate is **M** unless marked. G1–G3 unblock everything after them.

### G1 — Name the deep-link host and wire the app (TB-D4)

`contracts/app_links/terakidz.v1.json` has `base_status: placeholder` on
`https://app.terakidz.test`. The founder names the live host (the one that serves the SPA's
`/a/<id>` route), then in one reviewed commit: set `base` and `base_status: confirmed`, drop the
`base_note`, regenerate the images (above) and update `own-work.json`. The schema refuses a
confirmed `.test` host and a placeholder live host.

The native app must open the same links (operational notes — no code change in TB2):

- **Android:** an `<intent-filter android:autoVerify="true">` for `https://<host>/a/` in
  `android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml`, and `/.well-known/assetlinks.json` on the host with
  the release signing certificate's SHA-256.
- **iOS:** the Associated Domains entitlement `applinks:<host>` and
  `/.well-known/apple-app-site-association` on the host listing `/a/*`.
- **The app shell:** a `@capacitor/app` `appUrlOpen` listener that routes the URL's path into the
  router (B3 owns the SPA shell). `capacitor.config.ts` needs no change for app links.
- **Web:** the host serves the SPA with a history fallback so `/a/<id>` reaches the router.

### G2 — Replace the example copy, expert then founder

`templates/terakidz-box-insert/template.yaml` and the line say `EXAMPLE CONTENT`. A named
special-education reviewer (the B1 runbook's G1 names them) rewrites or approves the five tasks
against the physical box contents; the founder approves; the commit bumps the insert's `version`
and removes the marker (a reviewed change to the tests that pin it). The tasks must match what
is physically in the box.

### G3 — Register the QR images with their evidence

On the target instance, register each `asset:image-qr-terakidz-<id>` as a `factory.asset`:
kind `image`, licence `owned` (own work), commercial use, the author note and sha256 from
`own-work.json`, the file `own-work.json` as the licence evidence attachment, and the **real**
public URL of the hosted PNG (hosting is part of this gate; the harness uses a synthetic `.test`
URL). A second asset manager verifies each one (`factory-template-rollout.md` M3). "QR Code" is a
registered trademark of DENSO WAVE; the symbology itself is free to use.

### G4 — Suppliers and bill of materials (M)

Choose suppliers for the box, the physical task materials, the printed insert (A5, the QR images
at no less than 20 mm with their quiet zone) and the packaging. Record lead times, minimum order
quantities and unit costs; set the real box price (189 000 is a placeholder) and the product's
weight and dimensions for couriers. Add the components as purchasable products if assembly is
done in-house.

### G5 — Packaging and a print proof (M)

Order a proof of the insert and scan every QR on the proof with at least one Android and one iOS
phone before the print run: each must open its activity on the live host (G1). Packaging carries
the age band and the kit's disclaimer.

### G6 — Stock receipt (M)

Receive the first batch into the Terakidz company's warehouse through a purchase receipt (never
an inventory adjustment for a new product with a cost). The harness sells from zero stock; a real
order needs the box on hand before it is confirmed.

### G7 — Couriers and fulfilment (M)

Pick the courier integration and service levels, the packing and hand-over routine on the
delivery picking, returns handling, and who answers "where is my box" (KIDO/Chatwoot, CW1).

### G8 — Channels (R5 SHOP1 live)

The box reaches buyers through the web/Chatwoot intake (R1/CW1) and marketplaces (R5 SHOP1).
Marketplace listings, shop accounts and their order sync are R5's operational gates; B2 needs no
code for them. Every channel's order becomes a `sale.order` with origin `intake:<channel>`.

### G9 — Seller of record (R2), prices and live PAY1

The box is sold by the Terakidz legal entity (R2); set the price, tax and journal there. Paid
orders settle through PAY1 (`midtrans-rollout.md` stages 0–4, sandbox before production keys).

## Rollback

- **Content and links:** revert the TB2 commit(s) in kodemeio-dokploy; nothing on an instance
  changes until the insert is imported there. Printed boxes keep working as long as the host and
  the activity ids exist — **never rename or remove an activity a printed QR points at**
  (the SPA's snapshot test fails on that drift).
- **On an instance:** archive the box product (never delete one with sale lines); cancel
  unconfirmed box orders; revoke a QR asset only with a replacement insert ready.
- **App route:** removing `/a/:activityId` breaks every printed box; keep it for the life of the
  print run.

## Not in this runbook (deliberate)

- The product-line harness itself (kodemeio-odoo slice K2).
- B3 (the app's consent, deletion and entitlement) and its native shell wiring beyond the notes
  under G1.
- Per-box activation codes (TB-D4 (b)) — not built.
